title: "Don't let others control how YOU feel"
---

In my life, I don't think I've been bullied anymore than the average kid. I had a great childhood. But yet I still remember instances in my life from a really long time ago that kill me inside. Being told off by adults, being rude to others, etc. Right now its a tough patch for me that I hope will end. But it means I'm vulernable now. I let others decide how I feel, not myself. But why? Why give them the satisfaction? They don't even try to, I let them. Don't let them. Think positive thoughts.

Think positively, not negatively. You don't have to make everyone love you. You be yourself, don't think too much. Enjoy life, its short and you only get one.
